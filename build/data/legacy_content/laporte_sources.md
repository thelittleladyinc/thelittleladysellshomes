## History

- Laporte was permanently settled by French-Canadian fur trappers, with cabins along the Cache la Poudre River dating as early as 1828, making it the first white/Euro-American settlement in Larimer County — [Wikipedia: Laporte, Colorado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laporte,_Colorado); [Fort Collins Museum of Discovery](https://fcmod.org/trails/tour2/tour2-laporte.html); [SAH Archipedia](https://sah-archipedia.org/essays/CO-01-0001-0011-0005).
- Antoine Janis is credited as the first permanent settler, arriving 1844; the settlement was originally called "Colona," renamed Laporte in 1862 — [Wikipedia: Antoine Janis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antoine_Janis); [Fort Collins History Connection](https://history.fcgov.com/explore/county-history).
- Laporte was designated the first Larimer County seat in November 1861 by the Colorado territorial legislature; the seat moved to Fort Collins in 1868 — [Wikipedia: Larimer County, Colorado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larimer_County,_Colorado); [Fort Collins History Connection](https://history.fcgov.com/explore/county-history); [American Courthouses](https://courthouses.co/us-states/states-a-g/colorado-2/larimer-county/).
- Laporte has never incorporated and remains unincorporated Larimer County territory today — [Reddit r/FortCollins thread on Laporte history](https://www.reddit.com/r/FortCollins/comments/1ld057b/laporte_and_its_relationship_to_its_own_history/); [Larimer County LaPorte Area Plan PDF](https://www.larimer.gov/sites/default/files/uploads/2017/laporte_plan.pdf); [Apple Maps listing](https://maps.apple.com/place?auid=9568579292939472964).

## Geography / location

- Laporte sits on US-287, on the Cache la Poudre River, northwest of Fort Collins where the river emerges from the foothills — [Wikipedia: Laporte, Colorado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laporte,_Colorado).
- 2020 census population of Laporte CDP: 2,409 — [Wikipedia: Laporte, Colorado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laporte,_Colorado). (Not used as a stated statistic in the content per instructions against invented figures, but consulted for context.)

## Unincorporated status, housing, utilities, flood/fire

- Laporte is unincorporated; Larimer County (not a city government) holds land-use authority; described as the largest and most intensively developed unincorporated historic community in the county — [Larimer County LaPorte Area Plan PDF](https://www.larimer.gov/sites/default/files/uploads/2017/laporte_plan.pdf).
- Housing stock ranges from century-old homes to newly built homes, includes mobile/manufactured homes (20% of CDP housing units per the 2000 Census, per the plan) and large-acreage tracts — [Larimer County LaPorte Area Plan PDF](https://www.larimer.gov/sites/default/files/uploads/2017/laporte_plan.pdf).
- Water service is mixed: some areas served by City of Fort Collins or West Fort Collins Water District; many parcels on private wells — [Larimer County LaPorte Area Plan PDF](https://www.larimer.gov/sites/default/files/uploads/2017/laporte_plan.pdf).
- Septic (OWTS) systems require a Larimer County permit and soil/percolation testing; many Laporte parcels rely on septic rather than public sewer — [Larimer County Septic Systems (OWTS) page](https://www.larimer.gov/health/environmental-health/septic-systems/septic-systems-owts); [Larimer County LaPorte Area Plan PDF](https://www.larimer.gov/sites/default/files/uploads/2017/laporte_plan.pdf).
- FEMA-mapped floodplain/floodway along the Cache la Poudre River runs through Laporte; Larimer County prohibits new building envelopes within the 100-year floodplain — [Larimer County LaPorte Area Plan PDF](https://www.larimer.gov/sites/default/files/uploads/2017/laporte_plan.pdf); [Larimer County Zoning page (100-ft stream setback)](https://www.larimer.gov/planning/zoning).
- The Cameron Peak Fire (2020), the largest wildfire in Colorado history, burned in the upper Cache la Poudre watershed and led to evacuation orders reaching toward Laporte and use of Cache La Poudre Middle School as a Red Cross shelter — [Coalition for the Poudre River Watershed](https://www.poudrewatershed.org/cameronpeakfire); [CPR.org](https://www.cpr.org/2020/09/06/smoke-from-fire-burning-in-larimer-county-blankets-front-range/); [Denver7](https://www.denver7.com/news/local-news/mandatory-evacuations-ordered-in-cameron-peak-fire).
- A 2025 wildfire actually broke out within Laporte itself (4300 block of W County Road 54G), prompting brief mandatory evacuations — [Denver7](https://www.denver7.com/news/local-news/mandatory-evacuations-underway-in-laporte-due-to-wildfire-in-the-area); [CBS News Colorado](https://www.cbsnews.com/colorado/news/mandatory-evacuations-ordered-wildfire-burning-northern-colorado/). (Noted for context on fire risk; not cited as a specific named event in the content to avoid alarming specificity, but supports the general fire-risk statement.)
- September 2013 flooding significantly affected the Cache la Poudre River basin — [USGS Flood Report](https://pubs.usgs.gov/sir/2015/5119/sir20155119.pdf); [NWS Boulder](https://www.weather.gov/bou/Number1September2013Floods).
