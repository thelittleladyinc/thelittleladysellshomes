Why More Coloradans Are Choosing Cheyenne

By Christine Gwinnup · 2024-05-16

A Personal Milestone: My Journey to Wyoming

I wanted to share an exciting update with you: I've been a licensed real estate agent in Wyoming for two and a half years now, and it has been an incredible journey for so many reasons. The opportunity to help families find their dream homes in Wyoming has been both rewarding and eye-opening.

The Rising Costs in Colorado: A Growing Concern

One of the biggest factors driving this move has been the rising home prices in Colorado. Over the past few years, we’ve seen significant increases in property values, making it challenging for many families to keep up. For instance, median home prices in cities like Fort Collins and Loveland have soared, putting homeownership out of reach for many middle-income families.

Along with these escalating property values, Colorado residents are also grappling with higher property taxes. This increase in taxes can significantly strain household budgets, leaving less disposable income for other essential needs. Additionally, the cost of homeowner’s insurance in Colorado has been on the rise, partly due to natural disasters such as wildfires and hailstorms. These factors combined make it increasingly difficult to maintain a comfortable lifestyle in the state.

For many, the dream of homeownership is slipping away as monthly mortgage payments, property taxes, and insurance premiums continue to climb. This financial pressure is prompting many families to seek more affordable alternatives without sacrificing their quality of life.

Cheyenne, Wyoming: An Attractive Alternative

This is why I’ve seen a growing trend of people looking to move just 30 minutes away to Cheyenne, Wyoming. Cheyenne offers a fantastic alternative for those seeking more affordable living options while still being close to the vibrant culture and amenities of Northern Colorado.

Cheyenne's real estate market is notably more affordable. Lower home prices mean that families can purchase larger, more comfortable homes without stretching their budgets to the breaking point. Furthermore, Wyoming’s property taxes are among the lowest in the nation, offering immediate financial relief to new homeowners.

In addition to lower taxes, the cost of homeowner’s insurance in Wyoming is generally more reasonable. This is due in part to the state's lower incidence of natural disasters compared to Colorado. With more manageable insurance premiums, families can protect their investments without the financial burden they might experience in Colorado.

Cheyenne also boasts a friendly, tight-knit community, excellent schools, and plenty of recreational opportunities. The city’s proximity to Northern Colorado ensures that residents can still enjoy the cultural and social activities they love without a long commute.

In fact, I’m currently assisting a wonderful family in Loveland who are excited about their upcoming move to Cheyenne. They’re looking forward to enjoying the benefits of a more affordable lifestyle while still being able to visit friends and family in Colorado easily. They can maintain their connections while embracing the financial and lifestyle advantages that Cheyenne offers.
