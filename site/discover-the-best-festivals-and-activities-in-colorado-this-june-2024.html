Discover the Best Festivals and Activities in Colorado This June 2024

By Christine Gwinnup · 2024-06-03

Summer is here, and Colorado is brimming with exciting festivals and activities to enjoy throughout June. From art exhibitions and farmers markets to music festivals and outdoor adventures, there's something for everyone. Here’s a roundup of some of the top events you won’t want to miss.

Mile High International Pastel Exhibition

Date: TBD 2024
Address: Curtis Center for the Arts, Greenwood Village
About: Art lovers, mark your calendars for the Mile High International Pastel Exhibition at the Curtis Center for the Arts in Greenwood Village. This exhibition showcases nearly 100 pastel paintings, offering a feast for the eyes with stunning artworks from talented artists.

Highlands Farmers Market

Date: Sundays, May 19 – October 13, 2024
Address: Highland Square, 32nd Avenue, Denver
About: Enjoy a Sunday stroll through the Highlands Farmers Market, where you'll find handcrafted goods and locally grown produce. Arrive hungry and indulge in homemade foods that are fresh and ready to eat.

Civic Center EATS

Date: Wednesday and Thursday, early May to mid October, 2024
Address: Civic Center Park, Denver
About: Satisfy your taste buds at Civic Center EATS, a food truck extravaganza in Denver’s Civic Center Park. With 10 to 15 food trucks serving a variety of delicious meals, this event is a must-visit for food enthusiasts.

Mike the Headless Chicken Festival

Date: Fri-Sat, May 31-June 1, 2024
Address: Fruita
About: Celebrate the quirky and unique at the Mike the Headless Chicken Festival in Fruita. This two-day event features a 5k race, poultry show, disc golf tournament, and car show, all in honor of the famous headless chicken, Mike.

Greeley Blues Jam

Date: Fri-Sat, May 31-June 1, 2024
Address: Greeley
About: Experience the soulful sounds of blues music at the Greeley Blues Jam. This family-friendly festival offers a variety of events, and camping is available at Island Grove Park for a full festival experience.

Telluride Balloon Festival

Date: Fri-Sun, May 31-June 2, 2024
Address: Telluride
About: Witness the magic of hot air balloons at the 41st Telluride Balloon Festival. Enjoy two sunrise balloon ascensions from Town Park, a sunset glow on Main Street, and a balloon workshop.

Denver Chalk Art Festival

Date: Sat-Sun, June 1-2, 2024
Address: Golden Triangle, Downtown Denver
About: Watch over 200 artists transform the sidewalks of Golden Triangle into vibrant chalk masterpieces at the Denver Chalk Art Festival. This event is a visual treat for the whole family.

Animas River Days

Date: Saturday-Sunday, June 1-2, 2024
Address: Santa Rita Park, Durango
About: Celebrate the Animas River with a weekend full of kayak, raft, and SUP races at Animas River Days. The festivities conclude with a lively river parade and viewing party.

Colorado College Summer Music Festival

Date: Sun-Fri, June 2-21, 2024
Address: Colorado Springs
About: Enjoy three weeks of classical orchestral and chamber music at the Colorado College Summer Music Festival. This event showcases talented musicians from around the globe, along with Colorado College students and faculty members.

GoPro Mountain Games

Date: Thur-Sun, June 6-9, 2024
Address: Vail
About: The GoPro Mountain Games are the ultimate celebration of adventure sports, art, and music. Held annually in Vail, the event features competitions in biking, climbing, kayaking, and more, with activities for all ages and even events for dogs.

Colorado Medieval Festival

Date: Fri-Sun, June 7-9, 2024
Address: Loveland
About: Step back in time at the Colorado Medieval Festival in Loveland. Enjoy live entertainment, knights, swordsmanship, music, food, beer, craft vendors, and a kid zone. Note that no pets are allowed.

Colorado Shakespeare Festival

Date: Mixed days, June 8 – August 11, 2024
Address: Boulder
About: With over 60 seasons of success, the Colorado Shakespeare Festival in Boulder brings Shakespeare’s best plays to life. Performances are held on varying days on the CU campus, providing a cultural treat for theater enthusiasts.

FIBArk Salida

Date: Thur-Sun, June 13-16, 2024
Address: Downtown Salida
About: Join America’s oldest whitewater festival, FIBArk, in Salida. Dating back to 1949, this festival features thrilling events like the Slalom Race, Crazy River Dog, and the Hooligan Race.

Juneteenth Music Festival

Date: Saturday and Sunday, June 15-16, 2024
Address: Denver
About: Celebrate freedom and culture at the Juneteenth Music Festival in Denver. With live music, vendors, food stalls, art murals, activities for kids, and a parade, this festival attracts 50,000 attendees annually.

Strawberry Days

Date: Fri-Sun, June 21-23, 2024
Address: Glenwood Springs
About: The 127th annual Strawberry Days in Glenwood Springs is a weekend filled with live music, stage entertainment, a FamilyFest for kids, arts & crafts, a food court, a carnival, and a parade. Don’t miss the free strawberries and ice cream after the parade.

Greeley Stampede

Date: Thursday, June 26 – Tuesday, July 7, 2024
Address: Greeley
About: The 103rd annual Greeley Stampede features a PRCA rodeo, live country music, monster trucks, a demolition derby, carnival, roller derby, fireworks, and one of the largest Independence Day parades in the nation.

Fort Collins Festivals and Events

Garden Concert Series

Date: June through September, late afternoon/evening
Address: Locations vary by date
Cost: Varies by artist; $25-85
About: Enjoy live music in one of Fort Collins’ picturesque parks from June-September. The Garden Concert Series offers an intimate setting to experience a range of musical talents.

FoCo Food Truck Rally

Date: Every Tuesday from June 1 – September 14, 5:45 p.m. – 8:30 p.m.
Address: Fort Collins City Park
Cost: Free entry
About: Gather for live music and a selection of local food trucks Tuesday evenings at Fort Collins’ City Park. It's a perfect evening for foodies to explore diverse culinary delights.

Fort Collins Foodie Walk

Date: June 18, July 16, and August 20, 5 p.m. – 8 p.m.
Address: Downtown Fort Collins
Cost: Free
About: The Fort Collins Foodie Walk features Old Town’s finest culinary shops, staying open late on the 3rd Friday of each month for interactive showcases, seasonal flavors, and exciting taste experiences.

Loveland Festivals and Events

Movies on Main at Centerra

Date: June 25, 6:30 p.m. – 10:30 p.m.
Address: Main Plaza, Promenade Shops at Centerra
Cost: Free
About: Movies on Main is an outdoor movie series held on the Main Plaza. Enjoy a family-friendly film in a vibrant community setting.

With such a diverse array of festivals and activities, June in Colorado promises unforgettable experiences. Whether you're an art lover, foodie, music enthusiast, or adventure seeker, there's something for everyone to enjoy. Mark your calendars and get ready for a fantastic summer!

For more information on these events and to stay updated on the latest happenings, feel free to reach out to me. I’m here to help you explore the best of Colorado’s vibrant culture and exciting festivities!
