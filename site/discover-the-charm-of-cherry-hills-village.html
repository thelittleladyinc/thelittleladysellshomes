The History and Charm of Cherry Hills Village

Cherry Hills Village has a rich history dating back to the early 20th century. Originally a farming community, it was established as a village in 1945 and has since become one of the most sought-after residential areas in Colorado. The village takes its name from the abundance of cherry trees that once lined its streets, adding to its charm and allure.

Today, Cherry Hills Village maintains its small-town atmosphere, with tree-lined streets, spacious lots, and a strong sense of community. The village is known for its meticulously maintained properties and architectural diversity, ranging from historic homes to modern mansions. Residents take great pride in preserving the village's unique character and ensuring it remains a beautiful place to live.

Exploring the Different Neighborhoods in Cherry Hills Village

Cherry Hills Village is divided into several distinct neighborhoods, each with its own unique character and charm. Here are a few of the most popular neighborhoods in Cherry Hills Village:
Cherry Hills North: This neighborhood is known for its tree-lined streets and spacious properties. Homes in Cherry Hills North often feature classic architectural styles, such as Tudor and Colonial, and offer large lots and beautifully landscaped yards.
Buell Mansion: Located in the heart of Cherry Hills Village, Buell Mansion is an exclusive gated community that offers a luxurious and private living experience. The neighborhood features elegant homes and a variety of amenities, including a clubhouse, swimming pool, and tennis courts.

Cherry Hills Farm: Cherry Hills Farm is a prestigious neighborhood with a mix of traditional and contemporary homes. The neighborhood is known for its beautifully manicured streets and large lots, offering plenty of space and privacy.
Cherry Hills Park: Cherry Hills Park is an exclusive community that is home to some of the most luxurious estates in Cherry Hills Village. The neighborhood features expansive properties with impressive architecture and stunning landscaping.
No matter which neighborhood you choose, you can be sure that Cherry Hills Village offers a high standard of living and a strong sense of community.

Amenities and Attractions in Cherry Hills Village

Cherry Hills Village is not only known for its stunning homes but also for its abundance of amenities and attractions. Residents of the village have access to a wide range of recreational activities, making it the perfect place for those who enjoy an active lifestyle.
One of the most notable attractions in Cherry Hills Village is the Cherry Hills Country Club. This prestigious club offers world-class golf courses, tennis courts, swimming pools, and a clubhouse with fine dining options. In addition to the country club, Cherry Hills Village is home to numerous parks and open spaces. Residents can enjoy scenic walking trails, playgrounds, and picnic areas. For those who enjoy shopping and dining, Cherry Hills Village is just a short drive away from the bustling city of Denver. The city offers a wide range of shopping centers, restaurants, and entertainment venues, ensuring that residents have easy access to urban conveniences.

Top-Rated Schools in Cherry Hills Village

Cherry Hills Village is known for its excellent school system, making it a popular choice for families. The village is served by the Cherry Creek School District, which is widely regarded as one of the top school districts in the state.

Some of the top-rated schools in Cherry Hills Village include Cherry Hills Village Elementary School, West Middle School, and Cherry Creek High School. These schools offer rigorous academic programs and a wide range of extracurricular activities, ensuring that students receive a well-rounded education.

Families in Cherry Hills Village can have peace of mind knowing that their children are receiving a quality education in a safe and nurturing environment.
