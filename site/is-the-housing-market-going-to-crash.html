IS THE HOUSING MARKET GOING TO CRASH?

By Christine Gwinnup · 2022-07-01

The question on everyone's mind is
"IS THE HOUSING MARKET GOING TO CRASH?"

The very first thing I want to put out here is that if you are getting behind on your mortgage payments you are not alone.  I ask that you contact me asap, not necessarily to sell your home, but so I can help you navigate through the situation.  I have to tell you, I have already seen homeowners live in denial and loose hundreds of thousands of dollars because they went into foreclosure and didn't sell before the auction. Please don't let this be you or your loved one. I promise to do right by you and your family and friends and protect you through it to the best of my ability.

This post is not going to tell you the same that the majority of Real Estate Agents are saying, so please read on.

We have financial wizards telling us we are not in the same market that we were in 2007 and 2008.

We hear ideas such as... the supply of homes for sale remains at near all-time lows.

We hear that borrowers are more credit-worthy than ever before.

We are told to not believe the hype.

BUT, I personally know people that have lost their jobs. I know people that are selling their trailers, their boats, their HARLEYS and their homes preparing for what they see is the inevitable.

The truth is the market has started to shift. Prices on some houses have dropped. Homes seem to be staying on the market longer. There are less cash offers flying around.

Interest Rates continue to rise.

Selling and/or buying may not be right for everyone right at this moment. But it may be the best decision ever for some. The only way to know for sure is to set up a consultation with someone you trust and review the facts.

The way I see it, is there is opportunity in every market.

In my opinion, buying (the right home) is always better than renting. Refinancing down the road is a great option.

What I know is that real estate is the only financial decision that has held my head above water.

I have bought at the top and I STILL made money. I am currently searching for my own purchase, and considering selling.

Real estate can have short-term gain, but even better is the long-term payout.

I have been buying and selling homes on my own for almost 30 years now. I have been a landlord, I have done fix and flips, I have done airbnb, I have done lease with option to buy. I have made money by paying attention to the market and adjusting when need be.

If you want a Real Estate Agent that is going to sit down with you and make a plan to help you get into your perfect home and create wealth, not just sell your home and run, I'd love a chance to be your partner. Let's look at the facts and help you put more money toward your future because the 9-5, for most of us, just doesn't cut it.

If you want a realtor that is going to be honest about everything, especially about the current state of the real estate market, I’m your gal and I’m here to help.
