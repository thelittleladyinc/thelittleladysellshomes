The Best Places to Retire in Northern Colorado

By Christine Gwinnup · 2024-07-23

Retirement marks the beginning of a new adventure, and Northern Colorado is an ideal destination for retirees seeking a high quality of life. Here are the best places to retire in Northern Colorado, focusing on amenities, attractions, and lifestyle.

Fort Collins

Amenities: Fort Collins offers a mix of urban and outdoor living. The city boasts a lively downtown with restaurants, shops, and cultural attractions. The Fort Collins Senior Center provides activities for seniors, including fitness classes and art workshops.

Places to Visit:

  • Old Town Fort Collins: A vibrant area with shops, restaurants, and entertainment options.
  • Horsetooth Reservoir: Perfect for hiking, fishing, and boating with stunning views.
  • Fort Collins Museum of Discovery: An interactive museum perfect for all ages.

Lifestyle: The city's numerous parks and bike-friendly streets encourage an active and healthy lifestyle. The extensive trail system offers plenty of opportunities for outdoor recreation.

Loveland

Amenities: Loveland is known for its welcoming atmosphere and thriving arts scene. The Benson Sculpture Garden and Loveland Museum are cultural gems. The Chilson Recreation Center features swimming pools, fitness classes, and social clubs.

Places to Visit:

  • Benson Sculpture Garden: A beautiful park featuring a vast collection of sculptures.
  • Loveland Museum: Offers art exhibits, history displays, and cultural events.
  • Boyd Lake State Park: Ideal for water sports, picnicking, and camping.

Lifestyle: Loveland's proximity to the Rocky Mountains means outdoor adventures are just a short drive away. The city hosts numerous festivals and community events throughout the year.

Greeley

Amenities: Greeley combines small-town charm with big-city conveniences. The Greeley Senior Activity Center offers programs like educational classes and fitness activities. The historic downtown features unique shops and eateries.

Places to Visit:

  • Poudre River Trail: A scenic trail perfect for walking, biking, and enjoying nature.
  • Union Colony Civic Center: Hosts concerts, theater performances, and other cultural events.
  • Centennial Village Museum: A living history museum showcasing life in the late 1800s.

Lifestyle: Greeley’s affordable cost of living and friendly community are appealing. The city’s extensive park system and trails provide plenty of options for outdoor recreation.

Estes Park

Amenities: Estes Park, the gateway to Rocky Mountain National Park, offers breathtaking scenery and a close-knit community. The Estes Valley Community Center provides senior-focused programs and activities.

Places to Visit:

  • Rocky Mountain National Park: Offers stunning landscapes, hiking trails, and wildlife viewing.
  • Estes Park Aerial Tramway: Provides panoramic views of the surrounding mountains.
  • Downtown Estes Park: Filled with charming shops, restaurants, and galleries.

Lifestyle: Living in Estes Park means unparalleled access to outdoor activities such as hiking and wildlife viewing. The town’s charming downtown area with local shops and festivals adds to the appeal.

Windsor

Amenities: Windsor is known for its friendly neighborhoods and modern amenities. The Windsor Community Recreation Center offers fitness and recreational programs for seniors.

Places to Visit:

  • Windsor Lake: Ideal for fishing, boating, and community events.
  • Boardwalk Park: Features a museum, concerts, and family-friendly activities.
  • Pelican Lakes Golf Club: Offers beautiful golf courses and scenic views.

Lifestyle: Windsor’s golf courses, scenic trails, and open spaces make it a haven for outdoor enthusiasts. The town’s community events and activities foster a strong sense of community.

Northern Colorado offers a wealth of options for retirees, each community providing unique amenities, attractions, and lifestyle opportunities. Whether you prefer the city life of Fort Collins, the artsy vibe of Loveland, the charm of Greeley, the mountain beauty of Estes Park, or the community of Windsor, you’ll find a welcoming home in Northern Colorado.
