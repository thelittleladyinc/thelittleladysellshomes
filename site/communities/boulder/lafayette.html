Life In Lafayette

What It's Like To Live In Lafayette

Things To Do In Lafayette

Enjoy outdoor activities at Waneka Lake Park, a 147-acre recreational area featuring a 1.2-mile fitness trail, playgrounds, and opportunities for fishing and boating on the lake. Explore local history at the Lafayette Miners Museum, which showcases artifacts related to the city's coal mining heritage. Discover Lafayette's creative side with public art and murals throughout Old Town. Visit The Collective, Lafayette's community arts center, which hosts rotating art exhibits and workshops. Participate in Lafayette's annual Peach Festival each August, celebrating the region's peach harvest with food, music, and crafts.

Restaurants & Dining

Acreage by Stem Ciders offers high-elevation dining with mountain views alongside house-made ciders. Teocalli Cocina is a local favorite for authentic Mexican fare, and The Post Chicken & Beer is famous regionwide for its gluten-free fried chicken paired with local craft beer.

Dog Parks & Pet-Friendly Spots

Dog owners in Lafayette head to The Great Bark Dog Park, a fenced 6.1-acre off-leash area at 597 N. 119th St. with a separate small-dog section, open daily 6am-10pm.

Best Hikes & Trails Near Lafayette

The Waneka Lake Trail, the 1.2-mile loop around Waneka Lake, is an easy, scenic walk right in town with wildlife viewing at the adjoining Greenlee Wildlife Preserve. The Coal Creek Trail is a flat, paved multi-use path connecting Lafayette to neighboring Louisville and Superior.
