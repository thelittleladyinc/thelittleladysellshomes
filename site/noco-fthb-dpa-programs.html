“I’m Renting & Ready to See What’s Possible”

Perfect if you’re paying strong rent and wondering if you could own instead—without draining your savings.

  • Check $0-down and grant options.
  • See a realistic price band in today’s market.
  • Compare buying vs staying put for another year.
Path B • Already Own

“I Own—but the House No Longer Fits Me”

Ideal if you’re thinking of selling and buying again—downsizing, upsizing, or shifting towns within NoCo.

  • Explore selling with strategy (not panic).
  • See what your equity can actually do.
  • Look at downsize options, low-maintenance living, or acreage.
Path C • Relocating

“I’m Relocating to Northern Colorado”

Best if you’re moving for work, lifestyle, or family and want a clean, honest read on towns, prices, and timing.

Use the Advantages That Are Unique to Northern Colorado

Our market is different than Denver or the coasts. There are specific zip codes, pockets, and price bands where first-time and move-up buyers quietly win—if they know where to look.

Grants & Assistance (Beyond Google)

I track which Down Payment Assistance (DPA) and grant options are actually closing here—CHFA and beyond. Not everything you read online is practical in Larimer & Weld Counties.

  • See which programs match your credit + income band.
  • Understand where $0-down is realistic vs misleading.
  • Know which neighborhoods stretch your budget further.

Neighborhoods That Fit Your Season of Life

From downtown Loveland bungalows to quieter streets in Eaton or Windsor, the goal isn’t “any house”—it’s the right daily life.

  • Walkable vs quiet cul-de-sacs vs acreage.
  • Commute, schools, and weekend lifestyle reality.

Examples

Realistic Example Paths for NoCo Buyers

Track A • 0–3 Months

“We’re Ready If the Numbers Work”

You complete the Quick Check. Your credit and income look strong, and you’re flexible on town/neighborhood.

  • We confirm DPA / grant eligibility.
  • You get introduced to a local lender who works quickly.
  • Within a few weeks, you’re touring in 1–3 target areas.
Track B • 3–9 Months

“We Need a Short Prep Window”

Maybe credit needs a bump or you need a few months of job history. That doesn’t mean you’re stuck renting forever.

  • We build a simple 90–180 day plan.
  • You get lender guidance on what moves the needle.
  • We watch a short list of homes so you see real progress.
Track C • 6–12+ Months

“We’re Planning a Bigger Life Shift”

Big relocation, downsizing from a long-term home, or waiting on a life event? You still benefit from a plan now.

  • We clarify what you’re moving toward, not just away from.
  • You see what your current home could do for you.
  • We build a calm, smart timing window instead of a scramble.
