Discover Pierce, Colorado: A Charming Town on the Eastern Plains

By Christine Gwinnup · 2024-05-27

Nestled amidst Colorado’s Eastern Plains, Pierce is a charming small town located just 16 miles north of Greeley. Known for its tight-knit rural community, Pierce offers a serene lifestyle predominantly made up of farmers and ranchers, with a few modern businesses adding a touch of contemporary convenience in the town center.

Historical Charm of Pierce

The history of Pierce is deeply rooted in its establishment in 1918, when the town was officially incorporated. Named after General John Pierce, the president of the Denver Pacific Railroad, Pierce initially flourished as a subsidiary town of the railroad connecting Cheyenne, WY, to Denver. However, when the railroad operations moved out, many residents followed suit. Despite this, Pierce has managed to retain its historical charm, with several buildings from that era still standing.

Main Attractions in Pierce

Historical Buildings

Pierce’s uniqueness lies in its well-preserved historical buildings. Visitors can explore the old post office, general store, mercantile store—sometimes home to an antique shop—and the lumber yard, all offering a nostalgic glimpse into the town’s past. These buildings are a testament to Pierce’s rich history and provide a unique backdrop for those interested in exploring Colorado’s historical roots.

Song of the Lark Farm

For gardening enthusiasts, the nearby Song of the Lark Farm is a local gem. This large perennial nursery specializes in naturally grown, pesticide-free plants that thrive in the local climate. It's a perfect spot for those with a green thumb looking to add some native flora to their gardens.

Outdoor Recreation

Outdoor recreation in Pierce is limited, but Antelope Reservoir, located just three miles east of town, provides an excellent fishing spot. Spanning 47 miles, this reservoir is a tranquil destination for anglers and nature lovers alike. While Pierce may not have extensive recreational facilities, its close proximity to natural resources offers residents and visitors opportunities to enjoy the outdoors.

Proximity to Larger Towns and Cities

Pierce’s proximity to several larger towns and cities makes it a convenient location for those seeking a rural lifestyle with easy access to urban amenities. Nearby towns include:

  • Greeley (13 miles): A larger city offering a variety of shopping, dining, and entertainment options.
  • Fort Collins (21 miles): Known for its vibrant culture, breweries, and outdoor activities.
  • Loveland (33 miles): Offers a blend of art, culture, and outdoor recreation.
  • Longmont (53 miles): A city with a strong sense of community and numerous parks and trails.
  • Boulder (67 miles): Famous for its outdoor activities and vibrant downtown area.
  • Denver (75 miles): The state capital, offering a wide range of cultural and recreational activities.
