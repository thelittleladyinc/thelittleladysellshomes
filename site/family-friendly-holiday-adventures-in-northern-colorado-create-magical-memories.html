Family-Friendly Holiday Adventures in Northern Colorado: Create Magical Memories

By Christine Gwinnup · 2024-11-18

Northern Colorado during the holiday season is a dream destination for families seeking festive adventures and heartwarming experiences. With activities that delight kids and parents alike, the region offers something for every member of the family. Whether you’re looking for sparkling lights, playful outdoor fun, or cozy moments together, these destinations are sure to create lasting memories.

Estes Park: A Family Wonderland
Estes Park is a magical place for families during the holidays. Stroll through the twinkling streets with little ones sipping on hot cocoa from a local café. The annual Catch the Glow Parade is a must-see for kids, with brightly lit floats, marching bands, and holiday characters spreading cheer. Families can also explore Rocky Mountain National Park, where children will love spotting wildlife and playing in the snow. Many local accommodations cater to families, offering kid-friendly amenities and cozy spaces for evenings spent together by the fire.

Fort Collins: A Kid-Approved Holiday Adventure
Fort Collins’ festive atmosphere makes it a fantastic family destination. The Garden of Lights at the Gardens on Spring Creek will captivate children with its whimsical displays of illuminated animals, flowers, and holiday scenes. Old Town’s charming streets, decorated with thousands of twinkling lights, are perfect for a family stroll, and horse-drawn carriage rides add an extra layer of holiday magic. Local holiday markets often feature activities like cookie decorating, ornament crafting, and live music performances that kids will love.

Loveland: Festivities for All Ages
Loveland’s Winter Wonderlights at Chapungu Sculpture Park is an interactive wonderland for families, with synchronized music and dazzling light shows. For a quieter day, Boyd Lake State Park offers plenty of space for snowball fights, building snowmen, and winter picnics. Many of Loveland’s events include hands-on activities for children, such as meeting Santa or participating in holiday-themed scavenger hunts.

Greeley: Holiday Fun the Whole Family Will Love
Greeley is a treasure trove of holiday joy, especially for families with young children. The Festival of Trees features creative decorations that will capture kids' imaginations, while the Lights the Night Parade brings floats, performers, and plenty of holiday excitement to downtown streets. Parents will appreciate family-friendly performances at the Union Colony Civic Center, and local shops often feature special kid-friendly treats and activities.

Cheyenne, Wyoming: Old West Holiday Charm for Families
Just a short drive from Northern Colorado, Cheyenne offers a fun-filled holiday escape for families. The Old West Holiday celebration includes train rides perfect for kids who dream of a Polar Express-style adventure. The Cheyenne Christmas Parade is a hit with children, featuring festive floats, marching bands, and, of course, Santa Claus. Local museums often offer family-friendly exhibits and activities, giving kids a mix of holiday excitement and educational fun.

Tips for Traveling with Children During the Holidays

  • Choose Kid-Friendly Accommodations: Look for hotels or rentals with space for kids to play and unwind, as well as amenities like cribs or high chairs.
  • Pack Smart: Bring layers, hats, gloves, and boots for snowy adventures. Don’t forget favorite snacks and toys to keep kids happy during travel.
  • Plan Rest Stops: Break up long drives with planned stops at parks or family-friendly restaurants to let children stretch their legs.
  • Engage Kids in the Planning: Let children pick a holiday event or activity to build excitement and make the trip more enjoyable for everyone.

Northern Colorado is the perfect place to create magical family moments this holiday season. From sparkling lights to playful outdoor fun, you’ll find countless ways to keep your children entertained and your family connected.
