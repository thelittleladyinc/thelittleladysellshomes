Northern Colorado Day Trips from Loveland: Scenic Drives, Hikes & Local Gems

By Christine Gwinnup · 2025-08-14

Local Guide

Easy hikes, scenic drives, and small-town eats — all within 30–90 minutes. Save this list for weekends, visiting family, or when you just need mountain air.

Loveland Estes Park Rocky Mountain National Park Horsetooth Poudre Canyon Boulder Fort Collins

Perfect Weekend Day Trips Tip: If you prefer quieter trails and easy parking, go early (before 9am) or late afternoon, and check trail/park conditions before you go.

Estes Park & Rocky Mountain National Park (45–60 min)

Classic mountain town vibes, elk-spotting, and access to world‑class scenery in RMNP. Stroll the Riverwalk, grab a treat on Elkhorn Ave, then choose a short hike like Sprague Lake or Alberta Falls.

Quick Plan

  • Walk: Estes Riverwalk (easy, downtown loop)
  • Hike: Sprague Lake (0.8 mi, mostly flat)
  • Eat: The Stanley 

Good to Know

  • Timed Entry: RMNP often requires reservations in peak season.
  • Wildlife: Keep distance; bring binoculars.

Devil’s Backbone Open Space (10–15 min)

Right on Loveland’s west side: iconic rock formations, rolling trails, and wide‑open views. Great for golden hour photos.

Horsetooth Reservoir & Lory State Park (25–35 min)

Swim coves, paddleboard rentals, and mellow singletrack. Pack snacks and make it a half-day.

  • Trail: Arthur’s Rock (moderate, sweeping views)

Poudre Canyon Scenic Drive (45–60 min)

Follow the Cache la Poudre River up the canyon. Pullouts for picnics, photo ops, and short riverside walks.

Pack a lunch and follow the river.

Fort Collins Old Town (20–25 min)

Brick-lined streets, indie shops, and breweries. Family‑friendly, walkable, and photogenic.

Windsor Lake Loop (25–30 min)

Flat 2.25‑mile path around the lake, kayak and SUP rentals in season, and an easy coffee stop nearby.

Day Trip FAQ

Do I need a reservation for Rocky Mountain National Park?
Often in peak season (late spring–early fall). Check the official site and book ahead.
Are these trails family‑friendly?
Sprague Lake and Windsor Lake are very approachable. Always confirm current conditions.
Best time to avoid crowds?
Early mornings, weekdays, and shoulder seasons (late fall/early spring).
