Northern Colorado Day Trips from Loveland: Scenic Drives, Hikes & Local Gems

By Christine Gwinnup · 2025-08-14

Local Guide

Easy hikes, scenic drives, and small-town eats — all within 30–90 minutes. Save this list for weekends, visiting family, or when you just need mountain air.

Loveland Estes Park Rocky Mountain National Park Horsetooth Poudre Canyon Boulder Fort Collins

Perfect Weekend Day Trips Tip: If you prefer quieter trails and easy parking, go early (before 9am) or late afternoon, and check trail/park conditions before you go.

Estes Park & Rocky Mountain National Park (45–60 min)

Classic mountain town vibes, elk-spotting, and access to world‑class scenery in RMNP. Stroll the Riverwalk, grab a treat on Elkhorn Ave, then choose a short hike like Sprague Lake or Alberta Falls.

Quick Plan

  • Walk: Estes Riverwalk (easy, downtown loop)
  • Hike: Sprague Lake (0.8 mi, mostly flat)
  • Eat: The Stanley 

Good to Know

  • Timed Entry: RMNP often requires reservations in peak season.
  • Wildlife: Keep distance; bring binoculars.

Devil’s Backbone Open Space (10–15 min)

Right on Loveland’s west side: iconic rock formations, rolling trails, and wide‑open views. Great for golden hour photos.

Horsetooth Reservoir & Lory State Park (25–35 min)

Swim coves, paddleboard rentals, and mellow singletrack. Pack snacks and make it a half-day.

  • Trail: Arthur’s Rock (moderate, sweeping views)

Poudre Canyon Scenic Drive (45–60 min)

Follow the Cache la Poudre River up the canyon. Pullouts for picnics, photo ops, and short riverside walks.

Pack a lunch and follow the river.

Fort Collins Old Town (20–25 min)

Brick-lined streets, indie shops, and breweries. Family‑friendly, walkable, and photogenic.

Windsor Lake Loop (25–30 min)

Flat 2.25‑mile path around the lake, kayak and SUP rentals in season, and an easy coffee stop nearby.

Day Trip FAQ

Do I need a reservation for Rocky Mountain National Park?
Often in peak season (late spring–early fall). Check the official site and book ahead.
Are these trails family‑friendly?
Sprague Lake and Windsor Lake are very approachable. Always confirm current conditions.
Best time to avoid crowds?
Early mornings, weekdays, and shoulder seasons (late fall/early spring).

Things to do in Loveland itself — a local's short list

Before you leave town, know what's here: Loveland is Colorado's sculpture town — Benson Sculpture Garden alone holds well over a hundred pieces, and the annual sculpture shows draw artists nationally. Downtown's Fourth Street has become a genuine evening out — galleries, restaurants, the restored Rialto Theater. Outdoors, Boyd Lake State Park covers the water side; Devil's Backbone Open Space is the town's signature hike, that rock spine you see on half of Loveland's postcards; and the Loveland Recreation Trail loops the town for easy rides.

Seasonally: Valentine's season is Loveland's whole identity — the Sweetheart City remailing program stamps thousands of valentines every February — plus summer's outdoor concerts and the corn maze and pumpkin season out on the county roads. Living here means the 'day trip' question is often answered without starting the car — which, for what it's worth, is a big part of why my family stays.

The scenic drives worth a tank of gas

From Loveland you're positioned for some of the state's best drives: US-34 up the Big Thompson Canyon to Estes Park and Rocky Mountain National Park's alpine tundra; the Poudre Canyon (CO-14) west of Fort Collins, a state scenic byway tracing a wild and scenic river; the Peak to Peak Highway between Estes Park and Nederland for aspen season; and Horsetooth Reservoir's ridge roads for a sunset closer to home. Trail Ridge Road through the national park — the highest continuous paved road in the country — is the bucket-list one; it closes seasonally, so check before you go.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Loveland, Colorado known for?

Art — it's one of the country's notable sculpture towns — plus its Sweetheart City valentine tradition, Devil's Backbone Open Space, and its position as the gateway to the Big Thompson Canyon and Estes Park.

What are the best free things to do in Loveland?

Benson Sculpture Garden, Devil's Backbone and the Loveland Recreation Trail, downtown's public art and galleries, and lake evenings at North Lake Park cost nothing. Boyd Lake State Park charges a state-park pass.

What's the best day trip from Loveland?

Estes Park and Rocky Mountain National Park, forty-five minutes up the Big Thompson Canyon — with the Poudre Canyon a close second for a quieter, wilder river drive.

Is Loveland a good place to live?

I'm biased — I live here and sell homes here — but the combination of art scene, trail access, lakes, and a genuinely revived downtown at Northern Colorado prices is why people who visit keep asking me about listings.
