Are the lower interest rates on new construction really worth it?

By Christine Gwinnup · 2025-02-24

New construction homes continue to be a popular choice in **Northern Colorado**, but with rising home prices, shifting interest rates, and builder incentives, many buyers wonder: **Is a new build the right investment in 2025?**

Are New Construction Homes More Expensive Than Resale Homes?

  • Single-family new builds typically start around **$450K to $550K**.
  • Townhomes and paired homes can still be found **under $400K**, making them great for first-time buyers.
  • Resale homes in the same price range may offer **larger lots** or more established neighborhoods, but they may require updates or repairs.

Builder Incentives: Are They Worth It?

Many Northern Colorado builders are offering major incentives, such as:

  • **Mortgage Rate Buydowns** – Some builders cover part of your mortgage rate for the first few years.
  • **Closing Cost Assistance** – Builders may contribute thousands toward closing costs.
  • **Upgrade Credits** – Free appliance packages, premium flooring, or extended warranties may be included.

Customization vs. Move-In Readiness

New construction allows buyers to **customize** their home, including:

  • Floor plans and layouts
  • Cabinetry, countertops, and flooring
  • Exterior finishes and paint colors
  • Smart home features and energy-efficient upgrades

However, customization often **extends move-in timelines**. If you need a home **immediately**, a resale home or a **builder spec home** (move-in-ready new home) may be a better option.

Hidden Costs of New Construction Homes

  • **Landscaping** – Most builders do not include backyard landscaping.
  • **Window Coverings** – Many new homes do not come with blinds or curtains.
  • **HOA Fees or Metro Districts** – Some new communities have higher monthly costs.
  • **Fencing & Outdoor Upgrades** – Patios and extended driveways are often not included.

New Construction vs. Resale Homes: Which One is Right for You?

A **new construction home** may be the best choice if:

  • You want a brand-new home with **modern features**.
  • You prefer **customization and builder warranties**.
  • You qualify for **builder incentives that reduce costs**.

A **resale home** might be better if:

  • You need to **move quickly**.
  • You want **established landscaping and no extra costs**.
  • You prefer a **mature neighborhood with completed amenities**.
