Modern Convenience Meets Rural Living

By Christine Gwinnup · 2025-03-05

Experience True Colorado Country Living – 35 Acres with Expansive Views

Are you dreaming of owning land in Northern Colorado? Whether you are looking for privacy, space for animals, or a peaceful retreat with breathtaking views, this 35-acre property offers everything you need to embrace the best of Colorado country living.

Unmatched Views and Open Land

Nestled on 35 sprawling acres, this property boasts panoramic views stretching from Denver to Cheyenne. Imagine waking up to breathtaking sunrises, watching the weather roll across the plains, and enjoying the star-filled night sky free from city lights. If you are searching for a home with mountain views and wide-open space, this is it.

A Home Designed for Comfort and Functionality

This home blends modern convenience with rural charm. The gourmet kitchen features:

  • A massive island perfect for meal prep and gatherings
  • Double ovens for easy entertaining
  • An oversized pantry with ample storage
  • A luxury sink with premium attachments

The primary suite is a private retreat, complete with a spa-like five-piece bath featuring a soaking tub, spacious shower, and expansive vanity. A split-bedroom layout ensures privacy, with secondary bedrooms set apart from the primary.

Space to Expand and Create Your Dream Property

  • A 30x40 shop offers plenty of room for equipment, hobbies, or projects
  • A mudroom connects directly to the shop for seamless access
  • The expansive unfinished basement is already plumbed for an additional bathroom, ready to be customized

The Perfect Property for Homesteading or Ranching

Zoned for agricultural use, this property is ideal for horses, livestock, or a self-sustaining homestead. A private well provides a valuable water source, and the chicken coop allows you to enjoy fresh eggs every morning. If you are looking for a Northern Colorado property with acreage, this one checks all the boxes.

Modern Convenience Meets Rural Living

Unlike many rural properties, this home is equipped with fiber-optic internet, allowing you to work from home while enjoying country living. With the perfect blend of privacy, space, and connectivity, this home offers the best of both worlds.

If you have been searching for the perfect place to spread out and enjoy the beauty of Colorado, this 35-acre retreat is the one. Contact us today for more details.
