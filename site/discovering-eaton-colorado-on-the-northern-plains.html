Discovering Eaton, Colorado on the Northern Plains

By Christine Gwinnup · 2024-05-23

Nestled in the heart of Colorado's northern plains, Eaton is a charming statutory town in Weld County with a rich history and a promising future. Just over twenty miles east of Fort Collins, Eaton has grown from a modest settlement to a thriving community of nearly 6,000 residents as of the 2020 Census. This remarkable 32.92% population increase since 2010 is a testament to the town's appeal and potential.

A Glimpse into Eaton's Past

Eaton owes its name and much of its prosperity to Benjamin Harrison Eaton, a pioneering figure in the realm of irrigation. His visionary efforts in the late 19th century transformed the arid prairies east of Colorado's Front Range into fertile farmland. Today, Eaton's agricultural landscape continues to benefit from the irrigation systems he and his contemporaries established.

The town's official incorporation on October 27, 1892, was a community-driven effort led by 36 residents, including Eaton himself. Initially named Eatonton to avoid confusion with Easton in El Paso County, the name was later shortened to Eaton. The first settlers arrived in 1880, primarily working on irrigation projects between Eaton and Greeley, laying the foundation for a community that would flourish for generations.

A Strategic Location

Eaton's geographical coordinates, 40°31′46″N 104°42′47″W, place it along the Denver-Cheyenne mainline of the Union Pacific Railroad and U.S. Route 85, just 7 miles north of Greeley. This strategic location offers residents easy access to major urban centers while maintaining the charm and tranquility of a small town. Eaton is conveniently located within close proximity to several key cities:

  • Greeley: 8 miles
  • Evans: 11 miles
  • Garden City: 11 miles
  • Fort Collins: 23 miles
  • Loveland: 29 miles
  • Denver: 62 miles

A Growing Community

Eaton's population has seen significant growth over the decades, reflecting its increasing desirability as a place to live and work. From a humble 384 residents in 1900, the town's population surged to 5,802 by 2020. This growth has been accompanied by a rise in housing development, with 1,067 housing units reported in the 2000 Census.

The town's demographics paint a picture of a diverse and family-friendly community. With 36.7% of households having children under 18 and a median household income of $47,314, Eaton is an attractive place for families looking to settle in a safe and nurturing environment.

Embracing the Future

Eaton is not just about history and growth; it's also about community and opportunity. The town's high school, home to The Fightin' Reds, boasts notable alumni like Austin Ekeler, an NFL running back, and Trent Sieg, a long snapper, showcasing the town's talent and spirit.

With its elevation of 4,839 feet, Eaton offers breathtaking views of the surrounding plains and a comfortable climate that residents cherish. As part of the Greeley Metropolitan Statistical Area and the Front Range Urban Corridor, Eaton is poised for continued growth and development.
