Conestoga Subdivision Ault, Colorado

Welcome to Conestoga Subdivision in Ault, Colorado

Nestled in the heart of Weld County, the Conestoga Subdivision in Ault offers a perfect blend of suburban tranquility and modern amenities. Whether you're looking to buy your first home or seeking a vibrant community, Conestoga is the ideal place for creating lasting memories.

Exceptional Community Features

Conestoga is more than just a place to live—it’s a place to thrive. Residents enjoy access to well-maintained parks and a playground, adding to the area's family-friendly appeal. The community is managed by Pinnacle Consulting Group, ensuring top-notch services and amenities for all residents (Redfin).

Educational Excellence

Families in Conestoga benefit from the proximity to schools in the Ault-Highland RE-9 district. Highland Elementary, Middle, and High Schools are known for their commitment to excellence, offering a comprehensive education that prepares students for the future.

Local Amenities and Services

The Northern Plains Public Library, located at 216 W. 2nd Street, Ault, enriches community life by providing various educational programs, a gardening class, and even an indoor planetarium event. Its resources and activities are tailored to all age groups, from kids to adults (Northern Plains Public Library).

Planning and Zoning

The Town of Ault is dedicated to maintaining a safe, friendly, and attractive community. The planning and zoning policies ensure that Conestoga continues to be a well-tended neighborhood that adheres to the highest standards of social, economic, and environmental sustainability (Ault Colorado Gov).
