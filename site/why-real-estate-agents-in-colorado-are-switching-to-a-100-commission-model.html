Why Real Estate Agents in Colorado Are Switching to a 100% Commission Model

By Christine Gwinnup · 2025-10-26

If you’re a real estate agent in Colorado feeling stuck paying high splits, monthly desk fees, or signing up under a structure that doesn’t reward your effort, you’re not alone. Increasingly, agents are making the switch to a 100% commission brokerage model—and the reasons make sense. In this article, we’ll explore what a 100% commission model really means, why it’s gaining traction across the Front Range (Loveland, Fort Collins, Boulder), and how to evaluate whether the move makes sense for you. As Christine Gwinnup, RealTrends #2 Agent in Loveland (Transaction Sides 2025), I’ve seen this shift firsthand with LPT Realty.

What Does “100% Commission” Mean?

A 100% commission model means you keep the full commission from your deals, minus a flat transaction fee or cap, rather than splitting it with a brokerage (e.g., 70/30 or 80/20). Unlike traditional models where brokerages cover desk fees, admin, and marketing in exchange for a cut, 100% commission brokerages like LPT Realty shift those responsibilities to you—while offering free marketing and support to offset costs.

  • What You Keep: On a $500,000 home sale with a 3% commission ($15,000), a traditional 70/30 split nets you $10,500 after the brokerage takes $4,500. With LPT’s 100% option (e.g., $500/transaction fee), you keep $14,500—saving $4,000 per deal.
  • What the Brokerage Covers: LPT provides free marketing per listing (AI tools, print collateral), zero signup/monthly fees, and mentorship, reducing your out-of-pocket expenses.

Why Colorado Agents Are Switching Now

The shift to 100% commission is accelerating in 2025 for several reasons:

  • Cost Pressures: Rising living costs, marketing expenses, and advertising fees are squeezing agent profits. A 2025 survey showed Colorado agents spend an average of $1,200/month on marketing—LPT’s free offerings alleviate this.
  • Desire for Independence: Agents want to own their business, not just work for a brand. The Front Range’s competitive market demands flexibility.
  • Support Systems: Brokerages like LPT Realty offer no signup/monthly fees, free marketing per listing, and local mentorship—tailored to Loveland, Fort Collins, and Boulder. This support is a game-changer for scaling.

Key Factors to Evaluate Before You Make the Move

Before switching, consider these factors:

  • Fees & What’s Covered: Are there hidden fees? LPT has zero signup/monthly fees, but check for transaction fees (e.g., $500/file capped at $5,000/year). Included: free marketing, CRM, signage.
  • Support & Training: Is mentorship local? LPT offers weekly training and Colorado-specific onboarding—crucial for new agents.
  • Marketing & Brand: Free marketing per listing with LPT saves thousands versus agent-paid models at eXp or REAL.
  • Revenue Share / Residual Income: LPT’s uncapped 50% revenue share rewards recruiting, unlike capped models elsewhere.
  • Market Fit in Colorado Front Range: Loveland’s volume market and Boulder’s luxury niche both support 100% models with the right brokerage.

Common Questions (FAQ)

Q: Is this model riskier? A: It depends—if you’re producing consistent volume, you benefit more; if new, ensure strong support like LPT’s mentorship.

Q: Are there no fees at all? A: Some advertise zero signup/monthly fees, but always check contracts for transaction fees or subscriptions—LPT’s are transparent.

Q: How do I switch brokerages in Colorado? A: Notify your current broker, transfer your license, and evaluate the new contract. A 10-minute discovery call can streamline this.
