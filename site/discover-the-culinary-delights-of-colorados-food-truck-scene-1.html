Discover the Culinary Delights of Colorado's Food Truck Scene

By Christine Gwinnup · 2024-03-29

In the vibrant culinary landscape of Colorado, food trucks have carved out a special niche, offering an abundance of flavors that cater to every imaginable taste. From the heartwarming comfort of mac and cheese to the exotic allure of Cuban cuisine, these mobile eateries are redefining street food, one delicious bite at a time. Whether you're a local foodie or a visitor looking to indulge in Colorado's diverse food scene, there's no shortage of culinary adventures to be had. Spanning the diverse regions from the streets of Fort Collins to Loveland, Greeley, Longmont, Pierce, and Ault, there's a culinary adventure awaiting every local foodie and visitor alike. And with resources like Street Food Finder, tracking down these roaming kitchens has never been easier. Let's embark on a mouthwatering journey through some of Colorado's most enticing food trucks.

Mac'n: A Cheesy Delight on Wheels

First up is Mac'n, a food truck that turns the simple dish of mac and cheese into a gourmet feast. Imagine creamy, cheesy goodness topped with succulent pulled pork or other delectable toppings, making each bite a decadent experience. Mac'n showcases the versatility and universal appeal of mac and cheese, elevating it from a side dish to the star of the show.

Chefreys Cheesesteaks: Bringing the East Coast to the Rockies

Chefreys Cheesesteaks offers an authentic taste of Philadelphia right here in Colorado. Specializing in the classic cheesesteak, this food truck also ventures beyond to offer a variety of mouthwatering options that cater to a wide range of tastes. It's a slice of East Coast culinary tradition served with a side of Colorado flair.

Bigs Meat Wagon & BBQ: The Ultimate BBQ Experience

For those who crave the smoky, tender flavors of barbecue, Bigs Meat Wagon & BBQ is a dream come true. This food truck is a tribute to the art of BBQ, with slow-cooked meats that melt in your mouth and a commitment to quality that shines through in every dish. From brisket to ribs, it's a carnivore's paradise on wheels.

Babalu's Cuban Cuisine: A Culinary Journey to Cuba

Babalu's Cuban Cuisine transports diners to the vibrant streets of Havana with its authentic Cuban dishes. From the hearty ropa vieja to the iconic Cubano sandwich, this food truck offers a taste of Cuban culture and cooking. Babalu's invites you to embark on a flavorful journey, no passport required.

The Slawpy Barn: Comfort Food Classics

The Slawpy Barn delights with American comfort food favorites like burgers, fries, chicken nuggets, hot dogs, and brats. It's a nostalgic trip back to the basics, where simplicity and flavor reign supreme. This food truck proves that sometimes, the simplest dishes are the most satisfying.

Seven Fish: Sushi on the Go

Seven Fish brings the sophisticated flavors of sushi to Colorado's streets, offering fresh, quality ingredients in every roll. This mobile sushi bar is perfect for those looking to enjoy gourmet sushi in a casual, accessible setting. From classic rolls to innovative creations, Seven Fish is a testament to the art of sushi.

The Good Stuffed: Sweet and Savory Kolaches

The Good Stuffed captures the hearts of both sweet and savory food lovers with its innovative take on kolaches. Whether you're in the mood for a kolache filled with savory meats and cheeses or one stuffed with sweet fruit jams, this food truck offers the best of both worlds, wrapped in a soft, doughy embrace.

Los Altos Food Truck: Authentic Mexican Cuisine

Los Altos Food Truck stands out for its bold and vibrant Mexican dishes, bringing the flavors of Mexico to the Colorado streets. Offering everything from tacos and burritos to enchiladas and quesadillas, it's a celebration of Mexican culinary traditions, made with love and authentic ingredients.

The Snowy Churro: A Sweet Revolution on Wheels

In the heart of Northern Colorado, The Snowy Churro emerges as a delightful answer to gourmet dessert cravings, boasting an irresistible lineup of churros, edible cookie dough, sundaes, and floats. This mobile dessert oasis is a dream come true for those who cherish sweet indulgences, offering a refreshing twist on classic favorites. Whether you're savoring the crunch of a perfectly made churro or relishing the creamy texture of edible cookie dough, The Snowy Churro ensures that each bite is a flavorful celebration.

Nomad Street Cuisine: The Gourmet Road Warrior

Nomad Street Cuisine delivers a moving feast for the senses, offering a dose of gastropub sophistication on the go. With a menu featuring burgers, sandwiches, and hot dogs, this food truck elevates classic comfort foods with an innovative twist. The exceptional savory treats prepared by Nomad showcase the truck's dedication to quality and flavor, making it a prime destination for anyone eager to savor top-tier street cuisine.

The Aloha Life: A Frozen Oasis

In the warmth of Northern Colorado, The Aloha Life emerges as a refreshing haven, serving a wide variety of frozen treats destined to cool and delight. From exotic fruit popsicles to creamy soft serve, this food truck is a passport to a tropical paradise. The frozen delights of The Aloha Life are the perfect solution for anyone looking to satisfy a sweet craving while soaking in the relaxed atmosphere of Northern Colorado's vibrant food truck scene.

Angry Hippie Tacos: A Revolution on Wheels

Angry Hippie Tacos champions the farm-to-table movement with a Latin fusion twist, offering bold flavors and locally sourced ingredients. This food truck specializes in tacos that deliver a flavorful punch, embodying an eco-conscious philosophy that delicious food can also be sustainable. With a creative and appetizing menu, Angry Hippie Tacos invites food enthusiasts to enjoy a guilt-free culinary experience that's as responsible as it is satisfying.

Sweaty Moose: The Mountain's Culinary Delight

Sweaty Moose stands out as a beacon for those who adore hearty, soul-warming dishes. Specializing in mountain beans, nachos, fries, and corn fritters, this food truck captures the essence of mountain cuisine and brings it to the streets of Northern Colorado. Every dish is meticulously prepared, promising a taste of the highlands in each bite. The unwavering commitment to quality and flavor distinguishes Sweaty Moose in the vibrant Northern Colorado food truck scene.

Chaulkboard Gourmet Express: Classic American With a Twist

Chaulkboard Gourmet Express is transforming street food with its innovative fusion of American grill favorites and tacos. Esteemed throughout Northern Colorado for blending familiar tastes in novel and exciting ways, this food truck caters to both burger aficionados and taco enthusiasts alike, offering the best of both worlds in a single, convenient location.

BEDROCK BBQ: The BBQ Champion of Northern Colorado

Northern Colorado's barbecue scene is redefined by BEDROCK BBQ, a food truck renowned for its competition-style cooking and award-winning pitmasters. Specializing in succulent ribs, chicken, brisket, and pork, BEDROCK BBQ delivers an unparalleled BBQ experience. The commitment to excellence in flavor and quality is palpable with every mouthful, making it an essential stop for anyone passionate about true barbecue craftsmanship.

Colorado's Food Truck Tapestry

The food truck scene in Colorado is a colorful tapestry of flavors, cuisines, and cultures, reflecting the state's diverse and inclusive culinary landscape. These mobile kitchens not only offer a unique dining experience but also bring communities together, creating spaces where people can connect over their shared love of food. From comforting classics to exotic international dishes, Colorado's food trucks invite you on a delicious journey of discovery. So, follow your taste buds, explore the streets, and dive into the rich culinary world that these food trucks have to offer.
